Logicbroker marketplace integration

Business Systems Analyst · Integration · Vendor onboarding & process design

Onboarding wholesalers and retailers to the digital marketplace was slow and inefficient. The merchandising team relied on manual processes that took hours, and getting products online could take weeks because of limited development capacity. Logicbroker was selected as the integration solution to connect with FlexShopper's custom applications — the company's largest integration project at the time.

  • My role: Elicited requirements from operations, accounting and marketing; facilitated cross-departmental workshops; and proposed solutions for gaps Logicbroker could not address on its own.
  • Challenges: Produced detailed process documentation and an architecture diagram showing how Logicbroker would integrate, then designed custom integrations for major retailers that could not use the standard onboarding flow.
  • Results: Onboarding time dropped from several weeks to two weeks, contract-to-live. Standardized invoices let NetSuite automate wholesaler and retailer payments. Product-data enrichment was streamlined and merchandising effort reduced. 35+ vendors onboarded on the redesigned workflow.
  • Reflection: Strengthened my ability to manage conflicting stakeholder priorities while keeping solutions aligned to business goals, and deepened my process modeling and cross-team facilitation.

Rebate management app to streamline promotions

Business Analyst · Product design · Workshops & requirements

Rebate setup was slow and error-prone, requiring manual validation across multiple teams. The process delayed vendor promotion launches and introduced real risk of misconfiguration.

  • My role: Led the business analysis to design a custom rebate management application with a user interface for merchandising. Facilitated cross-functional workshops with developers, designers and merchandising stakeholders to define use cases, then delivered user stories covering every UI action and mapped example rebate criteria for both front-end and back-end logic.
  • Challenges: Supporting complex rebate criteria (UI rules, validation, change control), making sure SKU validation and error logging met developer requirements, and balancing merchandising usability against system functionality beyond rebate generation.
  • Results: Rebate setup dropped from one week to one day for the merchandising team and the rebate program scaled to more concurrent programs. The backlog work behind it also fed the wider merchandising roadmap.
  • Reflection: Sharpened my ability to balance stakeholder usability needs against technical system constraints so both business and development requirements were genuinely met.

New revenue channel via Catalog-as-a-Service (CaaS)

Business Analyst / Project Manager · Integration · Accounting & partner systems

FlexShopper needed new recurring revenue streams while keeping accounting accurate and complying with vendor policies, plus a way to syndicate its product catalog to merchant partner sites under a revenue-share model.

  • My role: Led both business analysis and project management. Gathered and defined requirements using accounting-workflow and custom CaaS application knowledge, then worked with development and accounting to design the process model and build a back-end automated invoicing system capturing revenue from third-party merchant sites. Coordinated requirements across Product, Sales, Legal, Marketing, Merchandising and Operations.
  • Challenges: Designing revenue-share processes that handled every return scenario by vendor policy, payment method and profitability tied to lease origination date — while staying scalable across partners without losing accounting accuracy.
  • Results: Opened a new recurring revenue channel through automated catalog syndication to partner merchant sites, with automated invoicing flows tied directly to partner transactions.
  • Reflection: Strengthened my ability to design at the intersection of business goals, accounting integrity and partner integrations, and to map complex financial workflows into scalable, system-driven processes.
